# Mind map — The Art of War (Sun Tzu)

To view: paste the block into mermaid.live or Obsidian.

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((The Art of War<br/>Sun Tzu))
    1 Laying Plans
      5 factors: the Way, Heaven, Earth, the Commander, Method
      Compare the forces before fighting
      All warfare is based on deception
    2 Waging War
      A long war exhausts the state
      Quick victory, not a long campaign
      Forage on the enemy's land
    3 Attack by Stratagem
      Winning without fighting is the ideal
      Order: attack the strategy, the alliances, the army, the cities
      Know the enemy and know yourself
    4 Tactical Dispositions
      First make yourself invincible
      Wait for the enemy's vulnerability
      Win before you fight
    5 Energy
      Direct and indirect forces
      Endless combinations
      Strength of the whole, not the individual
    6 Weak Points and Strong
      Arrive first and impose the ground
      Attack where the enemy does not defend
      Adapt like water
    7 Maneuvering
      Turn the detour into the direct route
      Banners, drums, communication
      Do not attack rested or motivated troops
    8 Variation in Tactics
      Some orders must not be obeyed
      Weigh advantage and risk
      5 fatal faults of the commander
    9 The Army on the March
      Read the signs of the terrain and the enemy
      Discipline with humanity
    10 Terrain
      6 kinds of terrain
      6 causes of defeat that are the commander's fault
      Care for soldiers as your own children
    11 The Nine Situations
      From dispersive ground to desperate ground
      With no way out, troops fight with everything
      Speed and surprise
    12 The Attack by Fire
      5 targets of fire
      Do not act out of anger
      Move only if there is an advantage
    13 The Use of Spies
      Information is worth more than battles
      5 kinds of spies
      The double agent is the most valuable
```

## Business translation

| Chapter | Principle | In business |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 factors | Diagnose before acting (morale, timing, market, leadership, structure) |
| 2 | A long war exhausts | Litigation and price wars burn cash; set a limit |
| 3 | Win without fighting | Agreement, partnership, or making the confrontation unnecessary |
| 4 | Invincible first | Guarantees, clauses, cash, an alternative (BATNA) before attacking |
| 5 | Direct and indirect | Main offer + side concessions/options |
| 6 | Strike the void | Niche, underserved customer, competitor's weak spot |
| 7 | Detour becomes shortcut | The longer route sometimes arrives first (e.g., partnership instead of direct entry) |
| 8 | 5 faults of the leader | Recklessness, cowardice, hot temper, pride, attachment — risks of whoever decides |
| 9 | Read the signs | The other side's behavior reveals the real situation |
| 10 | Terrain | Contractual position, jurisdiction, mutual dependence |
| 11 | Desperate ground | A team with no way out commits fully; use with care |
| 12 | Do not act out of anger | Emotional decisions destroy value |
| 13 | Information | Market intelligence is worth more than reaction |

Source: public domain text (trans. Lionel Giles, 1910 — Project Gutenberg). Original summary, not a transcription.
